Privacy Policy — US Visa Scheduler for Canada

Effective date: October 25, 2026.

Publisher: loose ends studio.

Privacy contact: browser.latte.1k@icloud.com.

This policy explains how US Visa Scheduler for Canada handles information when you use the Chrome extension. The extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with the U.S. government or the official visa scheduling provider.

Information processed on your device

The extension processes information from the official visa scheduling portal to carry out workflows you start: sign-in, availability monitoring, selected group and applicant validation, booking or rescheduling, and verification of appointment outcomes. This includes portal URLs and page content, account and group identifiers, applicant details, fee-payment status, consulate preferences, available dates and appointment details.

Your saved portal email and password, settings, availability observations, notification history, workflow checkpoints, pending booking verification and booking-success record are stored locally in your Chrome profile using chrome.storage.local. Temporary window and tab references use chrome.storage.session. Chrome Sync is not used. Storage access is restricted to trusted extension contexts.

Booking workflow records also retain normalized visa-class categories read from the selected group's applicant table. These optional categories are associated with the validated group and roster, not used to authorize booking.

View Logs also stores a local history of selected-consulate earliest-date changes, check times and booking outcomes. It retains at most 100 rows from the last 24 hours. Date history rows record which consulate each date was seen at. Internal comparison and attempt references are hashed; history records contain no credentials, raw visa/applicant identifiers, portal URLs or page content. History is not sent to analytics or the publisher.

A bounded internal diagnostics log supports reliability analysis of failed or recovered unattended checks. It keeps up to 1500 fixed developer event codes for up to 7 days outside the user interface: event code, timestamp, fixed validation-branch detail, workflow phase, run purpose, run mode, extension version and a failure count — never credentials, settings, portal URLs or page content, applicant identifiers, appointment dates or raw errors. It is not sent to analytics or the publisher automatically, and it is separate from the user-facing View Logs. On failure screens, Report a problem opens an email draft to browser.latte.1k@icloud.com with extension/browser versions, workflow mode, Test Mode, status, stage, a fixed failure category and recent sanitized diagnostics. Older entries may be omitted to fit email-link limits. You can review, edit or discard the draft; nothing is sent unless you send the email. Sending shares the report, your email address and any comments you add with the publisher through your email provider. An email app or configured browser email handler is required. Recording is best effort; the entry limit can remove older events sooner, and storage failures or a full recording queue can result in missing events.

Local storage is not an encrypted password vault. Someone with access to your device or Chrome profile may be able to read it. Protect your device and browser profile accordingly.

Apart from the verified booking date and normalized visa-class categories described below, portal information is not sent to the publisher or analytics provider. Credentials are never sent to the publisher or analytics provider; they are sent over HTTPS to the official scheduling provider when the extension signs you in. Booking actions send the necessary form data to that provider. The provider's own privacy policy applies to its services. The extension does not clear portal cookies or automatically sign you out.

Limited usage analytics

Configured production builds send five limited usage events to PostHog to understand adoption, workflow reliability and booking outcomes:

  • installation_seen: once per tracking-enabled installation.
  • workflow_started: when a new Book or Alerts workflow is accepted.
  • workflow_failed: when a tracked workflow ends with a terminal failure requiring user action.
  • alerts_monitoring_established: after a selected unpaid group and its first complete availability summary have been validated.
  • appointment_booked: after a real booking is verified as successful, once per tracked booking workflow; excludes Test Mode. Includes the exact booked calendar date and normalized visa-class categories, as described below.

Events contain random installation and event identifiers, a timestamp, extension version and production-environment label. Workflow events also contain a separate random session identifier, mode and Test Mode flag; failure events include a fixed failure category and predefined codes for the specific failure reason and workflow step. When known, they also identify the sign-in purpose (starting, renewing or verifying a workflow). Unavailable details are marked unknown or omitted. Failure details are limited status codes, not the internal diagnostics log, page content or raw error messages. These identifiers are not derived from your email, visa account or hardware. The installation identifier and a bounded queue of unsent events are stored locally.

Verified booking events additionally contain appointment_date, the exact booked calendar date in YYYY-MM-DD format, and visa_classes, a distinct list of predefined visa-class codes such as B1/B2, H1B, L1 or L2. These fields help the publisher measure how soon appointments are booked and compare outcomes by visa class. Classes are read from the selected group's dashboard applicant table and cross-checked during successful verification. A group may contain multiple classes; missing, unsupported or conflicting information is marked unknown. The event contains no per-applicant class mapping or applicant counts. Appointment clock times, previous appointment dates, visa-class descriptions and full confirmation details are not sent. Older queued booking events may lack the new fields; other usage events do not include them.

Analytics excludes passwords, email addresses, visa/group/applicant identifiers, applicant names, portal URLs, consulate and date preferences, availability details and appointment times. Apart from the exact verified booking date and normalized visa-class codes above, portal content is not included. The extension does not use session replay, heatmaps, autocapture, general browsing surveillance or automatic exception reporting. Ordinary polling, retries and periods with no available dates are not usage events.

The current release uses PostHog's U.S. ingestion endpoint at https://us.i.posthog.com. Source builds can instead select PostHog's E.U. endpoint or disable tracking. Development previews and test-mode builds do not send analytics. Enabling booking Test Mode in a configured production build blocks final booking submission; it does not disable that build's analytics.

Requests use HTTPS without cookies or a referrer. The extension sets the payload's IP field to null and disables GeoIP enrichment and person-profile processing. PostHog nevertheless receives the network connection's source IP. The project's Discard client IP data setting is currently disabled, so we do not promise that PostHog discards IP information. PostHog may process or retain technical connection information under its own policies. This tracking is pseudonymous, not fully anonymous.

The project currently uses PostHog's Free plan, which lists one-year event retention. PostHog describes this as a retention guarantee and says older data may be deleted; it is not a promise that every event is automatically erased exactly one year after collection. Provider logs, backups and other technical records are subject to PostHog's policies. See PostHog's retention information.

To request deletion of identifiable analytics records, contact the privacy address above. The publisher can seek assistance from PostHog to handle requests for these events, which are captured without person profiles. Deletion is not an immediate action available in the extension, and we do not promise an unverified processing deadline. The random installation identifier may be needed to locate the relevant events; an email address alone does not identify them.

For PostHog's own handling of data, see the PostHog privacy policy.

Optional payments

The optional Coffee feature opens Stripe checkout in a separate tab. The extension supplies only the selected amount in cents through a fixed payment link. It does not attach portal credentials, applicant or visa identifiers, appointment details, portal URLs, or analytics identifiers.

Stripe handles payment information and payment completion. The extension does not collect card details or determine whether you completed a payment. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Local settings and workflow records remain in your Chrome profile until they are overwritten or the extension is uninstalled. You can change saved credentials in Settings; this does not clear every stored record. There is no clear-all-data button in the first release. Uninstalling removes this extension's local storage from that profile and stops future extension activity. It does not delete records already held by the official portal, PostHog or Stripe.

View Logs hides rows after 24 hours and prunes expired history on extension wake, the scheduled cleanup alarm or a history read. Cleanup can wait while Chrome is closed or suspended; reopening the extension prunes expired rows. Unchanged availability can remain in the separate current-observation and workflow records described above.

The extension panel has no analytics opt-out switch. To stop future analytics, uninstall the extension or use a source build compiled with VITE_POSTHOG_ENABLED=false. A disabled build preserves its existing local analytics queue without sending it; re-enabling tracking may send those queued events. Installation tracking in a configured build may occur before you open the panel or start a workflow.

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact the privacy address above. Analytics identifiers are random and are not linked to your email, so we may be unable to identify records from your email address alone. The extension has no built-in remote-deletion request or installation-ID display. Please contact us before sending diagnostic information, and never send passwords, cookies, browser storage exports, applicant documents or unredacted appointment confirmations.

Other disclosures and security

The extension's publisher does not receive your portal credentials or operate a backend that stores your visa records. Analytics is limited to the events above. Local portal information is used to provide the monitoring and booking features you request, including safety checks and recovery after interruption.

The extension uses HTTPS and restricts portal actions to validated official pages and selected groups. These safeguards do not guarantee security or successful appointment changes. Always verify appointment outcomes directly on the official website.

The extension is not directed at children. Use it only with accounts you are authorized to manage.
